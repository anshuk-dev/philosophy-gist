## Frege: Sense (Sinn) & Reference (Bedeutung) (Western Philosophy)
**School:** Analytic Philosophy / Philosophy of Language | **Unit:** UNIT_04

### 🔗 Conceptual Chain (Dialectical Lineage)
- **1. Origin / Prior Knot:** Locke's Ideational Theory of Meaning (Words refer to private ideas)
- **2. Current Core Pivot:** **Meaning splits into the mode of presentation (Sense/Sinn) and the actual worldly object or truth-value (Reference/Bedeutung).**
- **3. Next Horizon:** Russell's Theory of Definite Descriptions (On Denoting)

### 🎯 The Fundamental Dilemma
> "How can an identity statement like 'The Morning Star is the Evening Star' be an informative discovery, while 'The Morning Star is the Morning Star' is a boring tautology?"

### 🏛️ Canonical Technical Lexicon
- **Sinn (Sense)**: The objective cognitive mode of presentation (Art des Gegebenseins) contained within an expression.
- **Bedeutung (Reference)**: The actual object picked out in the world, or the truth-value (True or False) of a sentence.
- **Context Principle (Kontextprinzip)**: Never ask for the meaning of a word in isolation, but only in the context of a proposition.
- **Truth-Value Gap**: Sentences containing non-referring terms have a sense but lack a truth-value for Frege.

### ⚔️ The Dialectic: Objection & Resolution
**Pūrvapakṣa (Opponent):** Mill claimed names are mere tags with denotation but no connotation. If 'Morning Star' and 'Evening Star' just denote the planet Venus, then 'a = b' says nothing more than 'a = a'.

**Siddhānta (Defense):** Frege proves in 1892: 'Morning Star' and 'Evening Star' have the IDENTICAL Bedeutung (Venus), but totally DIFFERENT senses (Sinn). Identity statements expand knowledge when they inform us that two different modes of presentation pick out the same worldly object.

### 📜 Primary Textual Locus
- **Author:** Gottlob Frege
- **Work:** *On Sense and Reference (Über Sinn und Bedeutung)*
- **Passage:** Zeitschrift für Philosophie und philosophische Kritik (1892): 25–50

### ⚠️ Central University Interview & UGC NET Trap
> What is the reference of a declarative sentence for Frege? The TRUTH-VALUE (The True or The False)! The sense of a sentence is the PROPOSITION or Thought (Gedanke). This is a standard UGC NET / Central University test question.

### 💡 Wisdom Gist
> "Words are not transparent glass. How we present a truth shapes what we understand. Two paths may lead to the same mountain, yet reveal completely different landscapes along the journey."
