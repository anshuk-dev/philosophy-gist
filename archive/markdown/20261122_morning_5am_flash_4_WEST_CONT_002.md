## Sartre: Existence Precedes Essence & Bad Faith (Western Philosophy)
**School:** Existentialism | **Unit:** UNIT_04

### 🔗 Conceptual Chain (Dialectical Lineage)
- **1. Origin / Prior Knot:** Heidegger's Thrownness (Geworfenheit) & Dasein's Finitude
- **2. Current Core Pivot:** **Human beings have no predetermined essence; we first exist, act, and define ourselves, condemned to be absolutely free.**
- **3. Next Horizon:** Postmodern Critiques of Subjectivity (Foucault's Death of Man & Power)

### 🎯 The Fundamental Dilemma
> "If God does not exist to design human nature like a craftsman designs a paper-knife, what grounds moral responsibility and human identity?"

### 🏛️ Canonical Technical Lexicon
- **Existence precedes essence**: Humans first encounter themselves in the world, and only through choices define who they are.
- **Être-en-soi (Being-in-itself)**: Non-conscious, solid, inert objects with fixed essences (a rock, a table).
- **Être-pour-soi (Being-for-itself)**: Consciousness characterized by lack, temporality, and radical freedom.
- **Mauvaise Foi (Bad Faith)**: Inauthenticity: lying to oneself by pretending to be a fixed object (en-soi) to evade the anguish of freedom.

### ⚔️ The Dialectic: Objection & Resolution
**Pūrvapakṣa (Opponent):** Religious and biological essentialists assert that human nature is predetermined by divine commandment, genetic makeup, or societal roles.

**Siddhānta (Defense):** Sartre declares in Being and Nothingness: 'Man is condemned to be free.' A waiter playing the role of a waiter too enthusiastically is acting in Bad Faith—pretending his career is an inescapable physical essence like the solidity of a rock. We are entirely responsible for our choices.

### 📜 Primary Textual Locus
- **Author:** Jean-Paul Sartre
- **Work:** *Being and Nothingness (L'Être et le Néant)*
- **Passage:** Part I, Chapter 2 ('Bad Faith') & 'Existentialism is a Humanism' (1946)

### ⚠️ Central University Interview & UGC NET Trap
> What is the crucial paradox of Bad Faith? In Bad Faith, the deceiver and the deceived are the EXACT SAME PERSON. It is not an ordinary lie told to others; it is consciousness using its own freedom to pretend it has no freedom.

### 💡 Wisdom Gist
> "There are no ready-made excuses. We cannot blame our past, our parents, or our circumstances for our character. Every morning, life asks us: who will you choose to be today?"
