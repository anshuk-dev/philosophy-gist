## Locke's Tabula Rasa & Primary vs. Secondary Qualities (Western Philosophy)
**School:** British Empiricism | **Unit:** UNIT_03

### 🔗 Conceptual Chain (Dialectical Lineage)
- **1. Origin / Prior Knot:** Cartesian Innate Ideas (Descartes & Leibniz)
- **2. Current Core Pivot:** **The mind is a blank slate (Tabula Rasa) furnished entirely by experience; reality splits into objective primary and subjective secondary qualities.**
- **3. Next Horizon:** Berkeley's Immaterialism (Dissolving primary qualities into secondary ones: Esse est percipi)

### 🎯 The Fundamental Dilemma
> "Where do our ideas come from if there are no innate truths stamped on the human soul at birth?"

### 🏛️ Canonical Technical Lexicon
- **Tabula Rasa (White Paper)**: The mind initially devoid of all characters, filled solely through Sensation and Reflection.
- **Primary Qualities**: Qualities inseparable from matter that resemble our ideas: extension, figure, motion, rest, solidity.
- **Secondary Qualities**: Powers in objects to produce sensations in us via primary qualities: colors, sounds, tastes, odors.
- **Substance (Something I know not what)**: The obscure unknown substrate required to support qualities.

### ⚔️ The Dialectic: Objection & Resolution
**Pūrvapakṣa (Opponent):** Rationalists argued that universal principles of logic (the law of identity) and moral ideas must be innate because children and animals cannot extract necessity from messy sensory instances.

**Siddhānta (Defense):** Locke in Book I of the Essay demonstrates that children and idiots do NOT possess the law of non-contradiction! If an idea were innate, it would be universally conscious. All complex ideas are constructed from simple ideas of sensation and internal reflection.

### 📜 Primary Textual Locus
- **Author:** John Locke
- **Work:** *An Essay Concerning Human Understanding*
- **Passage:** Book I, Chapters 1–2; Book II, Chapter 8 (§§ 9–26)

### ⚠️ Central University Interview & UGC NET Trap
> Interview boards frequently ask: 'What was Locke's test for distinguishing primary from secondary qualities?' Primary qualities survive physical division (divide a grain of wheat in half, each piece retains extension and solidity); secondary qualities change or vanish (almonds pounded in a mortar lose their color and texture).

### 💡 Wisdom Gist
> "We enter the world with minds open and ready to learn. Experience is the great teacher, and knowledge is built through patient, honest sensory engagement."
