## Satkāryavāda vs. Asatkāryavāda (Pariṇāmavāda vs. Ārambhavāda) (Indian Philosophy)
**School:** Sāṃkhya vs. Nyāya-Vaiśeṣika | **Unit:** UNIT_01

### 🔗 Conceptual Chain (Dialectical Lineage)
- **1. Origin / Prior Knot:** Epistemic validity of empirical observation (How objects change)
- **2. Current Core Pivot:** **The effect pre-exists in the material cause prior to production (Satkāryavāda), versus the effect is an entirely de novo creation (Asatkāryavāda).**
- **3. Next Horizon:** Cosmic Evolution & Dissolution (Sāṃkhya's 24 Tattvas & Advaita's Vivartavāda)

### 🎯 The Fundamental Dilemma
> "Does change create something genuinely brand new out of non-being, or does change merely unveil what was already latently present in the cause?"

### 🏛️ Canonical Technical Lexicon
- **Satkāryavāda (सत्कार्यवाद)**: The doctrine of existent effect: the effect is an unmanifest state of the cause.
- **Pariṇāmavāda (परिणामवाद)**: Real transformation: the cause undergoes real ontological modification into the effect (Sāṃkhya).
- **Asatkāryavāda / Ārambhavāda (असत्कार्यवाद / आरम्भवाद)**: The doctrine of fresh origination: the effect is non-existent before production (Nyāya).
- **Vivartavāda (विवर्तवाद)**: Illusory modification: the cause appears as effect without undergoing change (Advaita Vedānta).

### ⚔️ The Dialectic: Objection & Resolution
**Pūrvapakṣa (Opponent):** The Naiyāyika argues: If the cloth already existed inside the threads, the weaver's effort is pointless, and threads could clothe a shivering person. The cloth is a new substance inhering in threads.

**Siddhānta (Defense):** Īśvarakṛṣṇa in Sāṃkhyakārikā 9 proves Satkāryavāda: (1) Asadakaraṇāt (oil cannot be pressed from sand), (2) Upādānagrahaṇāt (specific materials needed for specific effects), (3) Sarvasambhavābhāvāt (not everything from anything), (4) Śaktasya śakyakaraṇāt (cause produces only what it has potential for), (5) Kāraṇabhāvāt (cause and effect share essence).

### 📜 Primary Textual Locus
- **Author:** Īśvarakṛṣṇa
- **Work:** *Sāṃkhyakārikā*
- **Passage:** Kārikā 9

### ⚠️ Central University Interview & UGC NET Trap
> Both Sāṃkhya and Advaita accept Satkāryavāda, but differ radically on transformation: Sāṃkhya asserts PARIṆĀMA (real, physical evolution of Prakṛti), while Advaita asserts VIVARTA (apparent, illusory superimposition on unchanging Brahman).

### 💡 Wisdom Gist
> "Nothing emerges out of sheer void. Creativity is not ex nihilo creation; it is the patient unfolding of inherent potentiality into vivid actuality."
