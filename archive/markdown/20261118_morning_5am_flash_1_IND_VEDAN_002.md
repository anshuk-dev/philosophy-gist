## Rāmānuja's Aprathaksiddhi & Saptavidhā Anupapatti (Indian Philosophy)
**School:** Viśiṣṭādvaita Vedānta | **Unit:** UNIT_01

### 🔗 Conceptual Chain (Dialectical Lineage)
- **1. Origin / Prior Knot:** Advaita's Nirguṇa Brahman & Māyāvāda
- **2. Current Core Pivot:** **Brahman is organically qualified by real matter (acit) and real souls (cit) through inseparability (Aprathaksiddhi); Māyā is logically self-contradictory in 7 ways.**
- **3. Next Horizon:** Dvaita Vedānta (Madhva's rejection of all non-difference in favor of Pañca-bheda)

### 🎯 The Fundamental Dilemma
> "Can non-dualism preserve the reality of individual souls, the external cosmos, and personal devotion (Bhakti) without dismissing them as illusory nescience?"

### 🏛️ Canonical Technical Lexicon
- **Aprathaksiddhi (अपृथक्सिद्धि)**: Inseparable relation: mode cannot exist without substance, yet is distinct from it (unlike Nyāya's Samavāya).
- **Śarīra-Śarīrī-Bhāva (शरीर-शरीरि-भाव)**: Body-Soul analogy: the world and individual souls constitute the real body (śarīra) of God (Brahman).
- **Saptavidhā Anupapatti (सप्तविधा अनुपपत्ति)**: Rāmānuja's 7 great incoherences of Advaita's Māyā: Āśraya, Tirodhāna, Svarūpa, Anirvacanīya, Pramāṇa, Nivartaka, Nivṛtti.
- **Viśiṣṭa-Aikya (विशिष्ट-ऐक्य)**: Non-duality of the qualified whole; Brahman qualified by subtle souls/matter becomes Brahman qualified by gross souls/matter.

### ⚔️ The Dialectic: Objection & Resolution
**Pūrvapakṣa (Opponent):** Śaṅkara argued that all differentiation is illusory; pure Nirguṇa Brahman alone is real, and any quality implies finitude.

**Siddhānta (Defense):** Rāmānuja in Śrībhāṣya dismantles Māyā: (1) Where does Māyā reside (Āśrayānupapatti)? Not in Brahman (pure light cannot host darkness) and not in Jīva (Jīva is produced by Māyā—circular reasoning!). (2) How can it veil self-luminous Brahman (Tirodhānānupapatti)? Veiling luminosity destroys its nature. Brahman is SAGUṆA, adorned with infinite auspicious qualities (kalyāṇa-guṇas).

### 📜 Primary Textual Locus
- **Author:** Rāmānuja
- **Work:** *Śrībhāṣya*
- **Passage:** Introduction to BS 1.1.1 (The Great Siddhānta / Mahā-siddhānta)

### ⚠️ Central University Interview & UGC NET Trap
> Compare Aprathaksiddhi (Viśiṣṭādvaita) and Samavāya (Nyāya): Samavāya relates two ALREADY established separate substances (ayutasiddha); Aprathaksiddhi relates an attribute that CANNOT even be conceived or exist without its substantive substrate.

### 💡 Wisdom Gist
> "The universe is not an empty illusion to be escaped; it is the living, breathing body of the Divine, worthy of reverence, care, and loving devotion."
