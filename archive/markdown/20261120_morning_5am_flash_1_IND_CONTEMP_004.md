## M. K. Gandhi: Satyāgraha, Ahiṃsā & Truth as God (Indian Philosophy)
**School:** Contemporary Indian / Moral Philosophy | **Unit:** UNIT_05

### 🔗 Conceptual Chain (Dialectical Lineage)
- **1. Origin / Prior Knot:** Machiavellian Political Realism (Ends justify the means)
- **2. Current Core Pivot:** **Means and ends are convertible; Truth (Satya) is God, and Non-violence (Ahiṃsā) is the only means to realize it.**
- **3. Next Horizon:** Contemporary Civil Rights Movements (Martin Luther King Jr. & Nelson Mandela)

### 🎯 The Fundamental Dilemma
> "Can politics and moral resistance be conducted without hatred, violence, and the utilitarian sacrifice of moral purity?"

### 🏛️ Canonical Technical Lexicon
- **Satyāgraha (सत्याग्रह)**: Soul-force or holding firmly to Truth; active non-violent resistance through self-suffering.
- **Ahiṃsā (अहिंसा)**: Active love and complete absence of ill-will in thought, word, and deed, even toward an oppressor.
- **Sarvodaya (सर्वोदय)**: The welfare and upliftment of all, prioritizing the last and least person (Antyodaya).
- **Swaraj (स्वराज)**: True self-rule: mastery over one's own desires and senses, beyond mere political transfer of power.

### ⚔️ The Dialectic: Objection & Resolution
**Pūrvapakṣa (Opponent):** Political pragmatists argued that non-violence is the weapon of the weak and helpless, utterly ineffective against ruthless imperial powers.

**Siddhānta (Defense):** Gandhi insisted in Hind Swaraj: Ahiṃsā is the weapon of the supremely brave! Striking an opponent is easy and animalistic; absorbing blows without retaliating or hating requires immense spiritual fortitude. By appealing to the opponent's conscience through self-suffering (tapasya), Satyāgraha converts rather than destroys.

### 📜 Primary Textual Locus
- **Author:** Mahatma Gandhi
- **Work:** *Hind Swaraj (1909) & From Yeravda Mandir*
- **Passage:** Hind Swaraj, Chapters 16 & 17 ('Brute Force' vs 'Soul Force')

### ⚠️ Central University Interview & UGC NET Trap
> Notice Gandhi's crucial shift in formulation: Early in life, he stated 'God is Truth'; later (1929), he radically reversed it to 'TRUTH IS GOD'. Why? Because even an atheist seeks truth; by defining Truth as God, no sincere seeker of truth is excluded.

### 💡 Wisdom Gist
> "The means are like the seed, and the end is like the tree. We cannot harvest peace by sowing seeds of bitterness and violence."
