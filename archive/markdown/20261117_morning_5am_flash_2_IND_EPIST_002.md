## Pratyakṣa: Nirvikalpaka vs. Savikalpaka & Ṣaḍvidha-Sannikarṣa (Indian Philosophy)
**School:** Nyāya-Vaiśeṣika | **Unit:** UNIT_01

### 🔗 Conceptual Chain (Dialectical Lineage)
- **1. Origin / Prior Knot:** Svataḥ/Parataḥ Prāmāṇya (Establishing whether perception is self-certifying)
- **2. Current Core Pivot:** **Perception begins as non-judgmental bare awareness (nirvikalpaka) before structuring into relational conceptuality (savikalpaka) via 6 types of contact.**
- **3. Next Horizon:** Buddhist Pramāṇavāda (Dignāga & Dharmakīrti rejecting Savikalpaka as pure conceptual fiction / Kalpanā)

### 🎯 The Fundamental Dilemma
> "How does sensory contact with an external object translate from a raw sensory datum into a structured, conceptual judgment ('This is a brown earthen pot')?"

### 🏛️ Canonical Technical Lexicon
- **Nirvikalpaka Pratyakṣa (निर्विकल्पक प्रत्यक्ष)**: Indeterminate, non-propositional perception apprehending bare structure without name or genus.
- **Savikalpaka Pratyakṣa (सविकल्पक प्रत्यक्ष)**: Determinate, propositional perception grasping the object qualified by name, genus, and attribute (viśeṣya-viśeṣaṇa-sambandha).
- **Ṣaḍvidha-Sannikarṣa (षड्विध सन्निकर्ष)**: The sixfold sense-object contacts: Saṃyoga, Saṃyukta-samavāya, Saṃyukta-samaveta-samavāya, Samavāya, Samaveta-samavāya, Viśeṣaṇa-viśeṣya-bhāva.
- **Alaukika Pratyakṣa (अलौकिक प्रत्यक्ष)**: Extraordinary perception: Sāmānyalakṣaṇa (genus), Jñānalakṣaṇa (complication/memory), Yogaja (intuitive gnosis).

### ⚔️ The Dialectic: Objection & Resolution
**Pūrvapakṣa (Opponent):** The Buddhist epistemologist (Dharmakīrti) argues: Perception is strictly non-conceptual (kalpanāpoḍham). The moment language, name, or universal is applied, it is no longer perception but mental construction (vikalpa). Savikalpaka is not perception at all.

**Siddhānta (Defense):** The Naiyāyika retorts: The universal (sāmānya) is a real objective entity inhering in the object. Savikalpaka perception directly sees the real substance as qualified by its real universal; words merely express what the sense faculty has already veridically grasped.

### 📜 Primary Textual Locus
- **Author:** Gautama & Annambhaṭṭa
- **Work:** *Nyāyasūtra & Tarkasaṃgraha*
- **Passage:** Nyāyasūtra 1.1.4 ('indriyārthasannikarṣotpannaṃ jñānam...'); Tarkasaṃgraha Dīpikā §30

### ⚠️ Central University Interview & UGC NET Trap
> How does Nyāya perceive absence (abhāva)? Through the 6th sannikarṣa: 'Viśeṣaṇa-viśeṣya-bhāva' (the absence of pot is an adjective qualifying the bare ground). Contrast this with Bhāṭṭa Mīmāṃsā, which invents a separate 6th pramāṇa: Anupalabdhi (non-apprehension).

### 💡 Wisdom Gist
> "Human perception is never merely passive recording; our cognitive faculties instantly weave raw immediacy into an intelligible, structured reality of meaning."
