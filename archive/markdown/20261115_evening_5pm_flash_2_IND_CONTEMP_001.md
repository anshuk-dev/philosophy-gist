## Sri Aurobindo: Involution-Evolution & The Supermind (Indian Philosophy)
**School:** Contemporary Indian | **Unit:** UNIT_05

### 🔗 Conceptual Chain (Dialectical Lineage)
- **1. Origin / Prior Knot:** Śaṅkara's Illusionist Māyāvāda vs Western Darwinian Materialism
- **2. Current Core Pivot:** **Matter is involved Spirit; cosmic evolution is the progressive ascent from Matter to Life, Mind, and Supramental Divine Gnosis.**
- **3. Next Horizon:** Teilhard de Chardin's Omega Point & Modern Transhumanist Philosophy

### 🎯 The Fundamental Dilemma
> "Can spiritual liberation be reconciled with terrestrial life and cosmic evolution without dismissing the world as an illusion or reducing mind to dead matter?"

### 🏛️ Canonical Technical Lexicon
- **Involution (Nimajjana)**: The prior descent and self-concealment of Spirit (Saccidānanda) into the density of inert Matter.
- **Evolution (Urdhvagati)**: The progressive ascent of consciousness unfolding what was secretly involved.
- **Supermind (Vijñāna / Truth-Consciousness)**: The intermediate divine gnosis linking transcendent Saccidānanda with the manifested cosmos.
- **Integral Yoga (Pūrṇa Yoga)**: Transformation of earthly human nature and divinization of life, rather than world-flight.

### ⚔️ The Dialectic: Objection & Resolution
**Pūrvapakṣa (Opponent):** Traditional ascetic Vedānta claimed the world is a dream to be abandoned (Māyāvāda), while Darwinian science claimed evolution is accidental, blind, and physical.

**Siddhānta (Defense):** Aurobindo proclaims: 'Evolution is an inverse action of the involution.' Life cannot evolve out of matter unless it was already involved within it; mind cannot evolve from life unless already hidden therein. The world is the delightful play (Līlā) of the Spirit ascending toward divine embodiment.

### 📜 Primary Textual Locus
- **Author:** Sri Aurobindo
- **Work:** *The Life Divine*
- **Passage:** Book I, Chapters 1–4 & Chapter 14 ('The Supermind as Creator')

### ⚠️ Central University Interview & UGC NET Trap
> Distinguish Aurobindo's evolution from Bergson's 'Élan Vital': Bergson's creative evolution is open-ended and non-teleological; Aurobindo's evolution is teleological and spiritual, anchored in the pre-existent descent (involution) of Saccidānanda.

### 💡 Wisdom Gist
> "The human intellect is not the ultimate peak of evolution, but an intermediate transitional instrument. The universe is slowly awakening to its divine source through us."
