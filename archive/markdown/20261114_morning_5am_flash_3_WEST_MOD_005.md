## Berkeley's Esse est Percipi & Immaterialism (Western Philosophy)
**School:** British Empiricism / Idealism | **Unit:** UNIT_03

### 🔗 Conceptual Chain (Dialectical Lineage)
- **1. Origin / Prior Knot:** Locke's Primary vs Secondary Qualities & Material Substance
- **2. Current Core Pivot:** **Matter does not exist; to be is to be perceived (Esse est percipi) or to perceive (Esse est percipere).**
- **3. Next Horizon:** Hume's Radical Skepticism (Applying Berkeley's critique to mental substance itself)

### 🎯 The Fundamental Dilemma
> "If secondary qualities exist only in the mind, how can primary qualities (shape, size) exist in an unthinking, unperceived 'material substance' outside mind?"

### 🏛️ Canonical Technical Lexicon
- **Esse est Percipi**: To be is to be perceived: sensible things are collections of ideas in minds.
- **Master Argument**: Can you conceive of a tree existing unperceived? The moment you conceive it, you are perceiving it in thought; unperceived matter is an impossibility.
- **Immaterialism**: The rejection of inert matter; reality consists of minds (spirits) and their ideas.
- **Divine Conservation**: Things don't vanish when we leave the room because God's omnipresent mind perceives the universe continuously.

### ⚔️ The Dialectic: Objection & Resolution
**Pūrvapakṣa (Opponent):** Locke and common sense argue: Even if color is subjective, the table's shape and solidity belong to the real physical object behind the sensory veil.

**Siddhānta (Defense):** Berkeley retorts: Try conceiving extension without color or texture! You cannot. Extension is known ONLY through visual or tactile sensations. If secondary qualities are mind-dependent, primary qualities must be equally mind-dependent. 'Material substance' is an incoherent philosophical phantom.

### 📜 Primary Textual Locus
- **Author:** George Berkeley
- **Work:** *A Treatise Concerning the Principles of Human Knowledge*
- **Passage:** Sections 1–33

### ⚠️ Central University Interview & UGC NET Trap
> Did Berkeley deny the reality of the physical world? Berkeley insisted he was defending common sense against skeptical materialists! He never denied that apples are crisp and stones hard; he only denied that they are made of unperceivable 'matter'. They are real, regular ideas caused by God.

### 💡 Wisdom Gist
> "The world is not a cold machine of dead matter spinning in empty space. Reality is an ongoing, luminous conversation between consciousness and creation."
