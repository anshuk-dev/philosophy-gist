## Aristotle: The Four Causes, Hylomorphism & Actuality/Potentiality (Western Philosophy)
**School:** Aristotelianism | **Unit:** UNIT_02

### 🔗 Conceptual Chain (Dialectical Lineage)
- **1. Origin / Prior Knot:** Plato's Transcendent Dual-World Theory of Forms
- **2. Current Core Pivot:** **Forms do not float in a separate heaven; they are immanent in matter (Hylomorphism), realizing potentiality into actuality.**
- **3. Next Horizon:** Medieval Scholasticism (Aquinas's Five Ways & Essence-Existence)

### 🎯 The Fundamental Dilemma
> "How does natural change occur without creating reality out of nothing or freezing reality into Parmenidean immobility?"

### 🏛️ Canonical Technical Lexicon
- **Hylomorphism**: Every concrete substance (ousia) is an indissoluble composite of Matter (potency) and Form (actuality).
- **Four Causes (Aitia)**: Material (bronze), Formal (statue shape), Efficient (sculptor), Final (telos/honor).
- **Dunamis vs. Energeia**: The acorn is potentially an oak tree; the mature oak tree is the realized actuality.
- **Telos (Teleology)**: Nature does nothing in vain; every entity is pulled forward by its inherent purpose or natural goal.

### ⚔️ The Dialectic: Objection & Resolution
**Pūrvapakṣa (Opponent):** Plato separated the Form from the concrete horse, creating an unbridgeable metaphysical chasm.

**Siddhānta (Defense):** Aristotle in Metaphysics Book VII argues: If forms exist separately, how do they cause change? Forms exist ONLY when instantiated in matter (except for the Unmoved Mover). Change is not impossible; change is the actualization of potentiality (energeia of dunamis).

### 📜 Primary Textual Locus
- **Author:** Aristotle
- **Work:** *Physics & Metaphysics*
- **Passage:** Physics II.3 (Four Causes); Metaphysics VII–IX (Substance and Actuality)

### ⚠️ Central University Interview & UGC NET Trap
> What is 'Primary Substance' (Prōtē Ousia) for Aristotle in the Categories vs Metaphysics? In the Categories, primary substance is the individual concrete particular (this man, this horse); in Metaphysics VII, it is identified with the essential Form that makes the matter this specific thing.

### 💡 Wisdom Gist
> "Growth is not an arbitrary accident. Within every seed, every thought, and every human being lies a slumbering potential waiting to flower into its true form."
